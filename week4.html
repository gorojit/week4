  2. Basic Geometry Formulas

    1. 2-D Formulas

      • Perimeter of Rectangle = 2(l + b)
      • Area of Rectangle = l × b

        →where: ‘l’ is Length , ‘b’ is Breadth

      • Area of Square = a2
      • Perimeter of Square = 4a

        • →Where ‘a’ is the length of sides of a Square
      • Area of Triangle= ½ × b × h

        • →Where ‘b’ is the base of the triangle and ‘h’ is the height of the triangle

      • Area of Circle = π × r 2
      • Circumference of Circle = 2πr

        • → Where ‘r’ is radius of a Circle

    2. 3 D Formulas

      • Surface Area of Cube = 6a2
      • Volume of Cube = a3

        • → Where ‘a’ is the length of sides of Cube

      • Curved Surface Area of Cylinder = 2πrh
      • Total Surface Area of Cylinder = 2πr(r + h)
      • Volume of Cylinder = V = πr2h

        • → Where ‘r’ is the radius of base of Cylinder ‘h’ is the height of Cylinder

      • Curved Surface Area of Cone = πrl
      • Total Surface Area of Cone = πr(r + l) = πr[r + √(h2 + r2)]
      • Volume of Cone = V = ⅓ × πr2h

        • → where, ‘r’ is the Radius of base of Cone h is the Height of the Cone

      • Surface Area of a Sphere = S = 4πr2
      • Volume of a Sphere = V = 4⁄3 × πr3

        • →Where, r is the Radius of Sphere



Chemical Equations

Basic Reactions:

  • Combustion:
    •
    • CH4 (g) + 2O2 (g) → CO2 (g) + 2H2O (g)
    • C3H8 (g) + 5O2 (g) → 3CO2 (g) + 4H2O (g)
  • Synthesis (Combination):
    •
    • 2H2 (g) + O2 (g) → 2H2O (l)
    • N2 (g) + 3H2 (g) → 2NH3
  • Decomposition:
    •
    • 2HgO (s) → 2Hg (l) + O2 (g)
    • CaCO3 (s) → CaO (s) + CO2 (g)
  • Single Replacement:
    •
    • Zn (s) + 2HCl (aq) → ZnCl2 (aq) + H2 (g)
    • Cu (s) + 2AgNO3 (aq) → Cu(NO3)2 (aq) + 2Ag (s)
  • Double Replacement (Precipitation):
    •
    • AgNO3 (aq) + NaCl (aq) → AgCl (s) + NaNO3 (aq)
    • BaCl2 (aq) + Na2SO4 (aq) → BaSO4 (s) + 2NaCl (aq)

More Complex Reactions:

  • Acid-Base Neutralization:
    •
    • HCl (aq) + NaOH (aq) → NaCl (aq) + H2O (l)
    • H2SO4 (aq) + 2KOH (aq) → K2SO4 (aq) + 2H2
  • Redox Reactions:
    •
    • 2Fe (s) + 3Cl2 (g) → 2FeCl3 (s)
    • 2MnO4− (aq) + 5SO32− (aq) + 6H+ (aq) → 2Mn2+ (aq) + 5SO42− (aq) + 3H2O (l)
  • Organic Chemistry Reactions:
    •
    • C2H4 (g) + H2 (g) → C2H6 (g) (hydrogenation)
    • CH3OH (l) + CH3COOH (l) → CH3COOCH3 (l) + H2O (l) (esterification)

Inorganic Reactions:

  • Metal Reactions:
    •
    • 2Al (s) + 3CuSO4 (aq) → Al2(SO4)3 (aq) + 3Cu (s)
    • Mg (s) + 2HCl (aq) → MgCl2 (aq) + H2 (g)
  • Non-Metal Reactions:
    •
    • S (s) + O2 (g) → SO2 (g)
    • 2H2S (g) + SO2 (g) → 2H2O (l) + 3S (s)
  • Gas Evolution:
    •
    • Zn (s) + H2SO4 (aq) → ZnSO4 (aq) + H2 (g)
    • NaHCO3 (s) + HCl (aq) → NaCl (aq) + H2O (l) + CO2 (g)

  • Fe + O2 → 4 FeO

  • 2 SO2 + O2 → 2 SO3
  • AgNO3 + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO3
  • Pb(NO3)2 + 2 KI → PbI2 + 2 KNO3
  • H2 + O3 → H2O + O2
  • 3 H2 + N2 → 2 NH3
  • 6 CO2 + 6 H2O → C6H12sub>O6 + 6 O2
  • 2 C12H23 + 35 O2 → 24 CO2 + 22 H2O
